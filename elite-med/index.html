the ER?

If it can't wait for an appointment, walk in. If it can't wait at all, call 911.

Walk in here

For illnesses and injuries that need care today but are not life-threatening. For example:

  • Coughs, colds and flu symptoms
  • Sore throats and earaches
  • A fever
  • Sprains, strains and minor falls
  • Small cuts, scrapes and minor burns
  • Rashes and bug bites

X-ray, point-of-care ultrasound and spirometry are on site.

Call 911 or go to the ER

For signs of a life-threatening emergency, like:

  • Chest pain or pressure
  • Trouble breathing
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or slurred speech
  • Bleeding that will not stop
  • Fainting, a seizure or sudden confusion
  • A serious head injury or a major accident

If this is a life-threatening emergency, call 911.

Not sure which one? Call (708) 741-7944 and ask before you come in.
