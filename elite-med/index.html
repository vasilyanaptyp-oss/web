About the clinic

A comfortable, patient-centered alternative to the ER

Elite Med Urgent Care is a walk-in clinic at 9634 S Roberts Rd in Hickory Hills. We see patients for illnesses, injuries and common conditions, with personal, patient-centered care.

For problems that are not life-threatening, a visit here can be a calmer option than the emergency room.

  • Accessible parking
  • Accessible restrooms
  • Free Wi-Fi
